Privacy policy

Doc Keeper stores your documents only on your phone. There are no Doc Keeper accounts or servers. The app connects to the internet only for purchases or the app update you choose.

Encryption. Document photos, PDFs and details are encrypted on the phone with a key that never leaves it.

Sharing. Quick share sends a document through your phone's own share sheet to the app you choose. Doc Keeper hands the file to that app and keeps no copy; the file is not encrypted once it leaves Doc Keeper.

App lock. If you turn on app lock, fingerprint, face and passcode checks are done by your phone itself. Doc Keeper only learns whether the check succeeded, never your fingerprint, face or passcode.

Updates. On Android, Settings can check GitHub for a newer version of the app and download it, only when you tap Check for updates. Like any download, the request shows GitHub your IP address. Nothing about your documents is sent, and Android asks you before installing.

No analytics. Doc Keeper collects no personal data or analytics, and sends no crash reports on its own. If the app closes unexpectedly, it keeps a short technical record on the phone (what went wrong, the phone model and app version, never your documents) and asks whether you want to send it to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you choose to, and you can read the report first in Settings → Problems.

Backups. Backups you export are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. Where you keep the file is up to you.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by the app store, only when you choose to see the price, buy or restore. Doc Keeper never sees your payment details. A completed unlock is saved on the phone and works offline.

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when you visit, as any web host does.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

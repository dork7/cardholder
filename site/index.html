What Doc Keeper does

  • Encrypted on the phone

    Photos, PDFs and details are sealed with AES-256-GCM. The key stays in the Android Keystore and never leaves the phone.

  • No account, no server

    Nothing is uploaded. There are no analytics and no ads. The app works without the internet.

  • Photos and PDFs

    Keep a front and back photo, or a whole PDF up to 10 MB. Share them to Doc Keeper straight from your gallery or files app.

  • Search what is printed

    The text on each document is read on the phone, so search finds names, numbers and other printed words.

  • Expiry reminders

    Get a daily notification before a document expires, starting 14 days ahead or the number of days you choose.

  • Ready at the counter

    Open a document full screen at full brightness and pinch to zoom.

  • App lock and blurred previews

    Ask for your fingerprint, face or passcode when the app opens, and blur a document's pictures on the home screen.

  • Encrypted backup

    Export everything as one file protected by a passphrase you choose, and keep it where you like.

  • Tags and a widget

    Group documents with tags, and list pinned ones by name on your home screen.

How to install Doc Keeper

  1. On your Android phone, open the latest release.
  2. Download DocKeeper-<version>.apk and open it.
  3. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or files app. Allow it and tap Install.
  4. To update later, open Settings → App updates → Check for updates in the app.

Doc Keeper is not on Google Play or the App Store yet. Updates are checked against their SHA-256 before Android installs them.

Questions

Does Doc Keeper upload my documents?

No. Documents are stored only on the phone. Doc Keeper has no accounts and no servers. It connects to the internet only when you tap Check for updates, or when you choose to see the price, buy or restore a purchase.

How does Doc Keeper protect my documents?

Every photo, PDF and detail is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a random key kept in the Android Keystore. Documents are left out of Google's device backups. With app lock on, the app asks for your fingerprint, face or passcode when it opens.

What happens to my documents if I lose or change my phone?

Documents exist only on the phone, so export a backup first from Settings → Export backup. The backup is one file encrypted with a passphrase you choose. Import it on the new phone with the same passphrase. A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.

Does Doc Keeper use AI or a cloud service to read my documents?

No. Text is read from photos and PDFs on the phone itself. The app has no language model and sends nothing to a cloud service.

Is Doc Keeper free?

Ten documents are free. Unlimited documents is a one-time purchase through the app store, not a subscription. Documents you already have are never locked.

How do I install Doc Keeper?

Download the latest DocKeeper APK from the project's GitHub releases page on an Android 10 or later phone, open it, and allow the install when Android asks. Doc Keeper is not on Google Play or the App Store yet.

Does Doc Keeper work on iPhone?

Not yet. Only the Android version is released.

Does Doc Keeper collect analytics or crash reports?

No. If the app closes unexpectedly it keeps a short technical record on the phone and asks whether you want to send it. Nothing is sent unless you choose to, and the record never contains a document.

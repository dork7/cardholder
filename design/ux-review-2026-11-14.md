# Doc Keeper UX/UI review — 2026-11-14

Reviewed at commit `102a874` (v1.13.0). Method: Nielsen heuristic evaluation, WCAG 2.1 AA audit mapped to React Native (screen-reader focus order in place of keyboard, TalkBack/VoiceOver semantics in place of ARIA, 44 pt touch targets), three user flows, cognitive load and form checklists. Source review by two independent passes, every severity-3 finding re-verified in the code, plus a runtime check of 200 % system text on the Pixel 9 Pro emulator. Target users: the general public, non-technical, all ages, in two situations: at home adding a document, and at a counter showing one in a hurry.

## Executive summary

The app is unusually careful about the things that are hard to retrofit: every destructive or lossy step confirms, contrast is unit-tested and the light palette was re-tuned to pass, reduce-motion is honoured everywhere, and the card stack is a model accessible control. The problems are concentrated at the two moments that matter most. At home, the first add stalls because photos must already be in the library and nothing says so. At the counter, finding an unpinned card takes up to nine swipes, the six actions on the card are icon-only, and the picture is half height when a back exists. Two defects are plain bugs: a dismissed share sheet is recorded as a successful backup, and clearing the reminder-days field blocks Save while the hint says the default applies.

## Accessibility compliance

- **Target:** WCAG 2.1 AA, native mapping.
- **Status:** of 13 criterion groups checked, 7 pass, 4 partial, 2 fail.
- **Failures:** 2.5.1 pointer gestures (pinch is the only way to zoom a document, `src/ui/ZoomableImage.tsx:22-40`); 1.3.4 orientation (portrait-locked everywhere except the card screen, `src/hooks/useAllowLandscape.ts:8-10`).
- **Partials:** 1.3.1 (no per-row names for Delete/Rename in Tags and Folders; PDF page radios report `selected` not `checked`); 1.4.4 (home screen cannot scroll, so large text can push the filters off small screens); 2.5.5 (tag sheet chips and Add at 36 pt, tag pills about 31 pt, stack dots 16 by 26 pt); 3.2.2 (OCR rewrites fields with no announcement).
- **Runtime check:** at 200 % system text the home and Settings screens reflow with no clipping. The native Settings header title does not scale. The "on" switch paints Android's default teal thumb because only the track colours are set (`app/settings.tsx:273`).
- **Passes with evidence:** contrast (`src/ui/__tests__/theme.test.ts`), use of colour, non-text content, focus order, forms, reduce motion, no font-scaling locks.

## User flow analysis

**(a) First run to first card from a photo.** About 12 steps including OS screens. Friction peaks at step 4: the user discovers there is no camera, leaves for the Camera app, and if app lock is on and the trip takes over 30 s, re-authenticates on return. Second peak at the silent smart-crop and the third-party cropper. The form then shows Card shape and Blur preview before Name. Likely drop-off: no photo ready.

**(b) At a counter, app lock on, find one of ten, show it, see the back.** Best case three taps if the card is pinned. Unpinned: the stack shows four, so the target can need up to nine swipes, a tap on a 6 pt dot, or the gallery toggle plus scrolling. The card view stacks front and back at half height each; the flip button exists only on the home stack. "Show to staff" is an unlabelled icon a first-timer will not find.

**(c) Free limit to purchase.** Six steps, three decisions. Price is hidden behind "See purchase price" by design. After "Done" the user is back on home and must tap + again. Every busy state shows "Contacting the store…".

**(d) Backup and restore.** Export: seven steps; the passphrase minimum is 3 characters with no reveal toggle, and the success alert fires whether or not the file was saved. Restore: eight to nine steps; errors are specific and promise nothing changed, but nothing at export time says where to keep the file.

## Top improvements, prioritised

1. **Record a backup only when it was written (sev 3).** Use `Directory.pickDirectoryAsync` plus `createFile`, already used for PDF export in `src/share/shareCard.ts:160-175`, as the primary "Save backup" path and set `lastExportAt` after the write. Keep "Send…" as a secondary option that records nothing.
2. **Treat an empty reminder field as the default (sev 3).** Map empty or whitespace to `DEFAULT_REMIND_DAYS` in `parseRemindDays`, and cap tag selection in the edit form at `MAX_TAGS` with the sheet's existing "Too many tags" alert.
3. **Label the card view's actions and demote editing (sev 3).** At most three labelled footer actions (Show to staff, Share, Edit); move Delete, PDF and Pin into Edit or an overflow; hide tag pills and the Details chip behind Edit. Rename "Details (.md)" to "Details", "Recreate .md" to "Read text again", "Save .md file" to "Save details as text".
4. **Make the counter path fast by default (sev 3).** Pin new cards by default as the design doc intends, or add a "Pin to home" toggle on the add form with a one-time hint. Show the back via the existing flip rather than two half-height images, or open landscape automatically when a back exists.
5. **Say that photos come from the library (sev 3).** One line on the empty state and in the Front field. Weigh adding the in-app camera that `react-native-image-crop-picker` already supports against the permission the design doc avoids.
6. **Give zoom a non-pinch path (a11y sev 3).** Make double-tap toggle 1× and 2.5×, add zoomIn/zoomOut accessibility actions, and optionally two 44 pt buttons.
7. **Unlock orientation outside the card screen (a11y sev 3)** and let the home screen scroll when content exceeds the viewport, so mounted phones and 200 % text both work.
8. **Show progress where work happens (sev 2).** "Preparing photo…" between picker and cropper, "Saving…" on the button, "Opening PDF…", "Loading…" instead of "No photo yet", and distinct paywall busy labels.
9. **Fix the small accessibility gaps in one pass (sev 2).** Per-row names for Delete/Rename in Tags and Folders; `checked` on PDF page radios; context in the Android expiry buttons ("Expiry date, 14 Nov 2026", "Clear expiry date"); announce OCR suggestions with a `LiveText`; 44 pt tag sheet chips and tag pill hit areas; correct the stack hint to "Swipe up or down".
10. **Unify destructive placement and wording (sev 2).** Delete last on every bar, "Cancel" instead of "Close" on the form bar, destructive styling on "Replace", locale-aware dates, "card" or "document" throughout.
11. **Trim Settings and the form (sev 2).** Collapse "Problems" to one row, move Blur preview and Card shape below Name, put Security first and purchases last, raise `MIN_PASSPHRASE_LENGTH` to 8 and add a reveal toggle.
12. **Cosmetic:** set `thumbColor` on switches; 3:1 outline on the round header buttons; dark-mode "!" on the expiry badge at 2.8:1.

## What works well

1. Discard protection is complete: Close, hardware back and swipe-down all route through `useConfirmDiscard` with "Keep editing" as the safe default.
2. The card stack is a model adjustable control: role, "Card i of n" value, increment/decrement/activate and a custom flip action, with expiry folded into the name.
3. Contrast is unit-tested and the light palette was re-tuned to pass; the `LiveText` abstraction gives every status message an announcement on both platforms.
4. OCR suggestions never overwrite anything the user touched, and reading never blocks the form.
5. Backup import validates all-or-nothing and its errors say exactly what happened and that nothing changed; the ID share confirmation, duplicate detection with "Keep both", and the app-lock-verified-against-device-lock check prevent the costly mistakes.

## Could not determine from code

Durations of smart-crop, thumbnail, PDF render, save encryption and PBKDF2; behaviour after Android process death while in the Camera app (form draft is in memory only); whether GitHub-release Android builds include Play billing; the third-party cropper's own labelling; whether share-in from Photos is wired (no usage of `strings.shareIn` found); where screen-reader focus lands after the tooltip modal closes; real-world contrast of white glyphs over user photos.

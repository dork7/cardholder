# Doc Keeper UX improvements

2026-11-15

## Summary

Doc Keeper is carefully built: thorough accessibility labels, 44pt touch targets, actions within thumb reach, and protection for unsaved edits. Two things stop it feeling fluid. Adding a document makes people leave the app to take the photo, and confirmation dialogs stand in for undo, so common actions get interrupted.

This review comes from reading the source of the home, card, edit, settings and notes screens. No device was available, so keyboard and TalkBack behaviour was checked through the code's labels and roles, not live. Severity uses Nielsen's scale: 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major, 4 must fix before release.

## Top 5 improvements

The first two give the biggest gain in ease of use for the least code.

1. **Take a photo inside the app (severity 3).** Add a "Take photo" button next to "From library" that opens the system camera through Android's `ACTION_IMAGE_CAPTURE` intent. The app never needs the `CAMERA` permission for this, as long as the manifest doesn't declare it. Then drop the "use your camera app first" hint.
2. **Undo instead of confirm (severity 3).** Delete straight away and show a 5-second "Deleted · Undo" bar, keeping the encrypted files until it times out. Use the same pattern for batch delete and tag removal. That removes three dialogs and the 2-second hold on tags.
3. **Fewer dialogs when sharing (severity 2).** Add "Don't ask again for IDs" to the ID warning. Let PDF export open the share sheet directly, since Android's sheet already offers saving to Files.
4. **Simplify the card screen's actions (severity 2).** Keep Show, Share and Pin visible. Move PDF, Edit and Delete into a "More" button (Delete is already on the edit screen). Make Close a small header button or rely on back, which frees about 60pt for the document.
5. **Show the gestures once (severity 2).** Add a one-time tip on the first stack ("Swipe to browse · tap to open") and in the first gallery ("Long-press to select"). Add captions to the selection bar's icons, as on the card screen.

## Adding a document

The main flow loses people at the start: the empty screen tells them to leave the app and take the photo elsewhere first.

| Step | Friction | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty screen says to use the camera app first | Has to leave the app | 3 |
| Camera app, take photo, come back | Switching apps; first-time users may lose their place | 3 |
| Add image, pick from library, crop | None | – |
| OCR fills the form, tap Create | None; the duplicate check helps | – |
| Notification permission asked after saving | None; asked once, in context | – |

**Fix that keeps the privacy rule.** `docs/design.md` rules out the camera permission, not a camera. Android's `ACTION_IMAGE_CAPTURE` intent hands off to the system camera and returns the photo without the app holding `CAMERA`, provided the manifest doesn't declare it (it is already blocked). On iOS the camera source of `UIImagePickerController` does need the permission prompt, so this fix is Android-only unless that prompt is acceptable.

## Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Accessibility is strong; three gaps remain, all small fixes.

**Done well:** labels on every icon button, live status text, screen-reader actions on the stack and grid (flip, blur, select), radio and checkbox roles on chips, 44–64pt touch targets, and contrast recorded in the theme (muted text `#5C5C5C` on white is about 6.4:1).

**Gaps:**

- **1.4.4 Resize text.** Card screen captions are 12pt on one line, in a row of 6 buttons about 50pt wide. At large system font sizes "Delete" and "Unpin" will cut off. Allow two lines, or move actions into a "More" button (improvement 4).
- **4.1.2 Name, role, value.** The home folder chip row is a radio group that also holds a "Manage" button, so screen readers announce a stray button among the radios. Keep action chips outside the group.
- **2.5.5 Target size.** The search clear button is 32pt plus an 8pt hit slop, 48pt in effect. It passes, but it is the smallest target in the app.

## Smaller improvements

These tidy up crowded screens and forms after the top 5 are done.

- [ ] **Merge the gallery's filter rows.** Gallery view can stack 3 chip rows (sort, folders, tags) plus search, the pinned strip and the action bar. Fold sort and "Expiring soon" into one "Sort" chip that opens a sheet.
- [ ] **Keep filters together.** Search sits at the top while tag and folder chips sit at the bottom; put them in one place.
- [ ] **Say what went wrong.** Replace the generic error with the cause and a next step, for example "Couldn't save the photo. Storage may be full."
- [ ] **Match the empty screen to the button.** Say "tap Add image" instead of "tap +".
- [ ] **Name the gallery toggle's target.** Label it "Stack" while the gallery is showing.
- [ ] **Offer reminder choices.** Replace the reminder-days number box with 7, 14, 30 or custom.
- [ ] **Polish the name field.** Add `autoCapitalize="words"` and `returnKeyType="done"`.

## What works well

Keep these as they are:

- Actions sit within thumb reach throughout: a floating action bar on home, and a save bar that stays above the keyboard with Close on the left and the main action on the right.
- Show-to-staff mode, with back locked and press-and-hold to exit, makes handing the phone over safe.
- OCR pre-fill never overwrites what the user typed, and the duplicate check offers to replace the old document.
- Back leaves selection first, the view and sort are remembered, and the stack resets when filters change.
- Screen-reader support goes beyond the minimum: actions for flip, blur and select, an adjustable stack, and live status text.
